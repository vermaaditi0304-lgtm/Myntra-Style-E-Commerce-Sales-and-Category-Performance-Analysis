# Fashion-E-Commerce-Sales-Category-Performance-Analysis

### Python | SQL | Power BI | Excel | Business Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end fashion e-commerce analytics project analyzing **5,000 Myntra-style transaction records** to evaluate sales performance, product categories, brand contribution, discount strategies, customer ratings, and order returns.

The project uses Python, SQL Server, Excel, and Power BI to transform raw data into actionable business insights supporting category management, pricing decisions, and sales optimization.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze category-wise and brand-wise revenue performance.
- Identify top-selling products and high-performing brands.
- Evaluate discount patterns and promotional pricing strategies.
- Examine monthly sales trends and order performance.
- Analyze customer ratings, returns, and refunds.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to monitor business KPIs.

## 📊 Dataset Description

**Dataset:** Myntra-style E-Commerce Transactions

**Total Records:** 5,000

**Key Features:**
- Order ID and Order Date
- Product, Brand, and Category
- MRP, Selling Price, and Discount Percentage
- Quantity and Revenue
- Customer Ratings and Review Count
- City and Payment Method
- Order Status, Returns, and Refund Amount

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales & Revenue Analysis
- Analyzed total revenue, total orders, and units sold.
- Evaluated average order value and monthly revenue trends.
- Identified top-performing products based on revenue.

### 2. Category & Brand Performance
- Compared revenue contribution across product categories.
- Identified leading brands and products.
- Examined category-wise sales distribution.

### 3. Pricing & Discount Analysis
- Analyzed average discounts across products and categories.
- Examined the relationship between discount percentages and sales.
- Evaluated promotional pricing patterns.

### 4. Customer Ratings & Returns
- Analyzed product ratings and customer review counts.
- Evaluated order return rates and refund amounts.
- Compared return patterns across categories.

## 💡 Business Insights

- **Category Performance:** Category-wise revenue analysis enables identification of high-performing product segments and their contribution to overall sales.
- **Brand Contribution:** Brand-level analysis highlights differences in sales performance, product demand, and revenue contribution.
- **Discount Patterns:** Comparing discount percentages with sales performance helps identify potential relationships between promotional pricing and revenue.
- **Sales Trends:** Monthly revenue analysis highlights seasonal fluctuations and changes in purchasing patterns.
- **Customer Engagement:** Product ratings and review counts provide indicators of customer engagement and product perception.
- **Returns & Refunds:** Return rates and refund amounts help identify product categories requiring further investigation.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- **Optimize Product Assortment:** Prioritize inventory planning and merchandising reviews for high-performing categories while evaluating opportunities in underperforming segments.
- **Improve Discount Strategies:** Compare discount levels with revenue and profitability to identify opportunities for more targeted promotional campaigns.
- **Strengthen Brand Performance:** Evaluate high-performing brands for assortment expansion and review low-performing brands for pricing or merchandising improvements.
- **Support Demand Planning:** Use historical sales trends to improve inventory allocation and prepare for seasonal demand fluctuations.
- **Enhance Customer Experience:** Monitor product ratings and customer feedback to identify potential product quality and satisfaction issues.
- **Reduce Returns:** Investigate categories with elevated return rates and evaluate improvements in product descriptions, sizing information, and quality control.



## 📌 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates an end-to-end e-commerce analytics workflow involving data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL querying, KPI development, and interactive Power BI reporting.

The analysis supports data-driven decision-making in fashion retail, particularly in **sales analytics, category management, pricing optimization, promotional analysis, and inventory planning**.
